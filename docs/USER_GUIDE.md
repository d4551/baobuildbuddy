# BaoBuildBuddy User Manual (Step-by-Step Quest)

Welcome to your BaoBuildBuddy campaign run. Each checkpoint below includes the actual UI screenshot plus a quick mission flow.

## 1) ASCII art banner (visual reference)

![ASCII art banner screenshot](./assets/user-guide/ascii-art-banner.png)

## 2) Home dashboard

![Home dashboard workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a474d762-2ffb-404d-9ace-f42c923b3db8)

1. Spawn in at `/`.
2. Review the quick-action and readiness cards.
3. Follow the highlighted "next quest" recommendation.

## 3) Settings

![Settings workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/68e8482f-641d-4b7c-8327-deb27e320b16)

1. Travel to `/settings`.
2. Choose your AI class (local endpoint or provider key).
3. Save and confirm your status indicators.

## 4) Resume builder

![Resume builder workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55100a10-79b8-43f9-a8f1-347773a68d43)

1. Open `/resume`.
2. Build or update your hero profile and experience.
3. Preview your loadout before job applications.

## 5) Jobs explorer

![Jobs explorer workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/627385cd-f332-4c0e-900c-957c6b5ddef5)

1. Open `/jobs`.
2. Search and filter open quests.
3. Save target roles to your pipeline tracker.

## 6) AI chat

![AI chat workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/997e8692-02ac-4fa0-9335-6b5c2708abfa)

1. Open `/ai/chat`.
2. Ask for strategy (resume, interview, outreach).
3. Reuse high-value responses in your workflow.

## 7) Automation hub

![Automation hub workflow screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72c3131c-032d-4a3c-a1b9-0af90c638943)

1. Open `/automation` (plus `/automation/job-apply` and `/automation/email`).
2. Start with non-sensitive sample data.
3. Track run history and evidence in the runs screens.
